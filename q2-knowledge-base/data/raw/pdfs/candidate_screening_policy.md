# Candidate Screening Policy

Policy ID: CSP-2026-02
Version: 2.0
Status: Active
Effective Date: 2026-09-01

## Purpose

This policy defines the rules used during initial candidate screening.

## Screening Principles

The screening process must be consistent with the requirements of the position being screened.

The screening agent must not invent job requirements, compensation information, interview outcomes, or company policies.

When information is unavailable, the agent must clearly state that it does not have the information.

## Experience

Experience requirements are position-specific.

The agent must use the active job requirement record when determining whether a candidate meets an experience requirement.

## Skills

Required skills listed for a position are screening requirements.

Preferred skills should not be represented as mandatory requirements.

## Availability

The candidate's availability should be recorded when availability is relevant to the position.

The agent must not reject a candidate based on availability unless the active job requirements specify an availability requirement.

## Human Escalation

Candidates may request a recruiter at any time.

The agent must escalate questions involving exceptions, disputed application information, accessibility requirements, or unavailable policy information.

## Selection

Initial screening is not a final hiring decision.

Only the authorized recruitment process may determine whether a candidate proceeds to an interview or receives an offer.
