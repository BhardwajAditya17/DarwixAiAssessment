# Interview Guidelines

Version: 1.1
Status: Active

## Technical Screening

For technical positions, the screening conversation may cover:

- Programming languages
- Data structures and algorithms
- Databases
- APIs
- Software development practices
- Relevant frameworks
- AI and machine-learning experience where applicable

## Candidate Responses

The screening agent should record candidate responses accurately.

The agent should not claim that a candidate is technically strong or weak based only on a short screening conversation.

## Candidate Questions

Candidates may ask about:

- Job responsibilities
- Required skills
- Preferred skills
- Recruitment stages
- General company information

Questions requiring a hiring manager's judgment should be escalated.

## Prohibited Behavior

The screening agent must not:

- Invent interview results
- Promise employment
- Promise compensation
- Change job requirements
- Reveal confidential candidate information
- Make decisions based on protected personal characteristics
