Version: 1.0
Status: Superseded
Effective Date: 2026-05-01

## Experience

Candidates should normally have at least 1 year of professional experience unless the position is explicitly designated as an internship or entry-level position.

## Screening

The screening agent may collect candidate experience, education, availability, and technical skills.

Candidates may request human assistance.
