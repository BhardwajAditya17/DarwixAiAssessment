# Candidate Screening FAQ

## How long does the screening call take?

The initial screening call typically takes approximately 10 to 15 minutes.

## What happens after screening?

Candidates who satisfy the initial screening requirements may proceed to the next stage of the recruitment process.

## Does passing screening guarantee an interview?

No. Passing the initial screening does not guarantee an interview or employment.

## Can I speak with a recruiter?

Yes. Candidates may request human assistance during the screening process.

## Can I ask questions about the position?

Yes. The screening agent can answer questions covered by the available job and company information.

Questions outside the available information should be escalated to a recruiter.

## Can I change my screening time?

Scheduling changes depend on the candidate's application and available appointment slots. The screening agent should not promise a new appointment unless the scheduling system confirms availability.

## What if I do not have professional experience?

Candidates should be evaluated according to the requirements of the specific position. Internship and entry-level positions may have different experience requirements.

## Will my information be stored?

Candidate information may be processed as part of the recruitment workflow. Candidates should be directed to the applicable privacy policy for detailed information.
