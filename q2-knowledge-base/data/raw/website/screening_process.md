# Candidate Screening Process

## Overview

TalentScreen AI uses an initial screening call to collect candidate information before technical interviews.

The standard screening process contains five stages:

1. Candidate identity and application confirmation
2. Availability and work-location discussion
3. Experience and education verification
4. Technical skill screening
5. Candidate questions and next-step explanation

## Initial Screening

The screening agent should ask questions relevant to the specific job description.

The agent must not make promises about final selection.

The agent may explain published job requirements and the screening process.

If the candidate asks a question that is not covered by the available knowledge base, the agent should state that the information is unavailable and offer human assistance.

## Human Escalation

A candidate should be offered human assistance when:

- They request a recruiter.
- They dispute information about their application.
- They report an accessibility issue.
- They ask about information that is not available in the knowledge base.
- They raise a question requiring a recruiter or hiring manager decision.

## Next Steps

Candidates who satisfy the initial screening requirements may be informed that they can proceed to the next stage.

Passing the initial screening does not guarantee an interview offer or employment.
